# Mohammed-Abdu-Arena
Tensile structure project for Mohammed Abdu Arena in Riyadh, showcasing CForce’s expertise in architectural membrane structures and large-scale entertainment venues.
Mohammed Abdu Arena – Riyadh
Where Architecture Meets Entertainment

Mohammed Abdu Arena is one of Riyadh’s prominent entertainment venues, hosting major concerts and events within Boulevard Riyadh City. The arena has become an important destination for large-scale entertainment experiences in Saudi Arabia.

CForce contributed to the development of the arena through its expertise in tensile architecture and advanced membrane structures, supporting the creation of functional and visually distinctive architectural elements.
